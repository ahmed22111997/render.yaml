Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Podcast TTS Studio ("the Site", "we") collects when you use the service, and how it's handled.

Content you submit

The text you type into the script field is sent to a third-party text-to-speech service in order to generate audio. We do not require an account, and we do not permanently store the scripts you submit.

Generated audio files

Each episode you generate (MP3/WAV, and SRT subtitles if requested) is stored temporarily on our server so you can download it, and is automatically deleted a few hours after creation. We do not publish, share, or review the audio you generate.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data and manage your ad preferences at Google's Advertising Policies page.

Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand overall traffic (for example, page views), without identifying individual visitors personally.

Third-party service providers

Audio generation relies on a third-party neural text-to-speech provider. Submitted text is transmitted to that provider solely to produce the requested audio, subject to that provider's own terms.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
